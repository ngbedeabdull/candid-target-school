Academic Excellence & Curriculum

A rigorous dual framework harmonizing national educational standards with 21st-century global competencies.

Academic Structure

Curriculum Divisions

Carefully staged learning trajectories designed to support adolescent cognitive growth from foundational discovery to specialized mastery.

Junior Secondary (JSS 1 – JSS 3)

Foundational Knowledge & Discovery

The Junior Secondary phase establishes profound mastery in fundamental core competencies: mathematical logic, linguistic expression, introductory scientific inquiry, and digital literacy. Culminating in the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

Core Subject Offerings:

  • Mathematics & Reasoning
  • English Language & Literature
  • Basic Science & Technology
  • Social Studies & Civic Edu
  • Agricultural Science
  • Information & Comm. Tech
  • French & Indigenous Lang
  • Creative & Cultural Arts
Senior Secondary (SSS 1 – SSS 3)

Specialization & University Preparation

Senior students select tailored pathways based on career aspirations: Pure Sciences, Commercial Studies, Humanities, or Applied Technology. Prepares learners for WAEC, NECO, JAMB/UTME, and international SAT/IELTS assessments.

Specialized Pathways:

  • Physics, Chem, Biology
  • Further Mathematics
  • Financial Accounting & Econ
  • Government & Civic Studies
  • Literature-in-English
  • Technical Drawing & Coding
  • Commerce & Marketing
  • Geography & Data Proc
Academic Faculty

Subject Departments

Department of Sciences

Igniting Curiosity Through Practical Experimentation

Our science department trains aspiring doctors, aerospace engineers, pharmacists, and research scholars. Every theoretical concept is reinforced with mandatory weekly bench laboratory investigations in our modern science wing.

  • Core Disciplines: Pure Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Agricultural Science, Further Mathematics.
  • Special Programs: National Olympiad Coaching, STEM Innovation Fair, Astronomy & Rocketry Circle.
  • Faculty Lead: Mr. Babatunde Lawal (M.Sc. Applied Physics).
99.2%

Distinction rate in WAEC & NECO Science subjects over the past five consecutive sessions.

Department of Arts & Humanities

Articulate Thought, Cultural Literacy & Eloquence

Fostering deep analytical reading, rhetorical finesse, creative writing, and legal foundations. Our humanities scholars frequently represent the state in national debate rounds and Model United Nations summits.

  • Core Disciplines: Literature-in-English, Government, History, Christian & Islamic Religious Studies, French.
  • Special Programs: Annual Shakespeare Play, Parliamentary Debate Society, Press & Journalism Bureau.
  • Faculty Lead: Mrs. Fatima K. Bello (M.A. English & Literature).
#1

State Debate Champions for three consecutive academic tournaments.

Department of Commercial Studies

Financial Acumen, Trade & Enterprise Leadership

Equipping future corporate executives, accountants, chartered bankers, and entrepreneurs with rigorous financial literacy, market analysis, and ethical commerce standards.

  • Core Disciplines: Financial Accounting, Commerce, Economics, Bookkeeping, Marketing & Salesmanship.
  • Special Programs: Student Investment Club, Mock Stock Exchange Challenge, Young Entrepreneurs Venture Pitch.
  • Faculty Lead: Mr. Chinedu Eze (B.Sc., ACA Chartered Accountant).
100%

Success in professional foundation examinations and national commerce evaluations.

Department of Technology & Vocational

Coding, Applied Engineering & Creative Craft

Bridging intellect with practical dexterity. Students master algorithmic thinking, python coding, computer-aided design (CAD), electronics, and vocational culinary arts.

  • Core Disciplines: Computer Studies & Data Processing, Technical Drawing, Basic Electricity, Foods & Nutrition.
  • Special Programs: Python & Web Development Bootcamp, Arduino Robotics Lab, Graphic Design Workshop.
  • Faculty Lead: Engr. Samuel Oladipo (B.Eng. Computer Engineering).
30+

Student-built web and robotic prototypes showcased at annual Innovation Expos.

Admissions & Enrollment

Your gateway to purposeful scholarship and leadership. Discover our streamlined application process.

Clear Steps to Join

The 4-Step Admission Journey

We ensure a transparent, candid, and seamless experience for prospective scholars and their families.

1

Inquiry & Tour

Submit an online inquiry form, download the school prospectus, or book a guided tour around our academic campus.

2

Application Form

Purchase and complete the official admission application dossier with past academic transcripts and birth certification.

3

Exam & Interview

Candidates sit for the Entrance Assessment (Math, English, General Aptitude) followed by a short candidate & parent interview.

4

Formal Enrollment

Successful applicants receive official admission offers, uniform fittings, boarding allocation, and induction pack.

Criteria

Entry Requirements

We welcome candidates who exhibit intellectual curiosity, sound moral deportment, and eagerness to excel.

✓
Junior Secondary (JSS 1):

Candidate must have successfully completed Primary 6 (or equivalent) and be at least 10–11 years old by September of the admission year.

✓
Transfer Candidates (JSS 2 & SSS 1):

Official continuous assessment reports and cumulative transcripts from the previous accredited secondary school, plus a letter of good conduct.

✓
Documentation Needed:

4 recent passport photographs, medical immunization report, certified birth certificate, and proof of residence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding admissions deadlines, entrance examinations, and boarding life.

Registration for the main national entrance assessment closes on July 31, 2026. However, rolling supplementary assessments are conducted for mid-stream transfer scholars subject to seat availability in our class rosters.
Prospective Junior Secondary students are evaluated in Mathematics, English Comprehension & Grammar, Verbal/Quantitative Reasoning, and General Elementary Science. Senior Secondary candidates write papers matched to their chosen academic stream (Sciences, Commercial, or Arts).
Yes, we offer both Day and Full Boarding accommodations. Our modern hostels feature 24/7 security surveillance, resident housemasters, structured evening study hours, and hygienic dining services overseen by professional nutritionists.
The Founder’s Merit Scholarship is strictly performance-based. The top three scorers in our nationwide entrance examination are automatically interviewed by the Board of Governors for full or 50% tuition coverage for their three-year Junior cycle.
